ANALYSIS

Sample Data

Analytics

These trend graphs are sample signals for a selected joint. They help operators understand whether the joint is stabilizing, degrading, or approaching a fault condition.

Health Index Shows the overall condition of the joint across recent passes. A steady fall often signals rising wear, alignment loss, or thermal stress.
Temperature Tracks heat buildup at the splice. Faster climbs usually indicate friction, poor contact, or a developing hotspot.
Vibration Measures mechanical impact and belt instability. A rising curve often points to mechanical looseness or uneven joint loading.
Load / Tension Displays the force acting through the belt. Sudden increases can reveal overload conditions, drift, or abnormal stress distribution.
